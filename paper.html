Embroidery Grammar: From Modular to Network Systems

Abstract. This study investigates the structural transformation of blackwork embroidery from modular systems to network-based configurations. Using BW-046, BW-065, and WWB-082 as progressive case studies, we demonstrate a shift from simple geometric repetition to layered structural logic. The embroidery process is analyzed through three dimensions: structure, path, and tension. The results reveal that WWB-082 represents a network-based embroidery system with distributed tension and hierarchical execution.

Comparison

BW-046 represents a basic modular system. BW-065 extends into a structured combinational system, while WWB-082 evolves into a network-based system with layered execution and distributed tension fields.

Conclusion

WWB-082 demonstrates a fundamental shift in embroidery logic, where structure precedes path optimization, and tension is distributed across multiple layers. This confirms the emergence of embroidery as a formalizable computational system.
